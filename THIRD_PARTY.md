# Research attribution and third-party materials

Dream Lab is an independent implementation inspired by **Dream-RSI: Recursive
Self-Improvement through Evolving Worlds**, Tong Zheng and coauthors.

- [Original research repository](https://github.com/zhengkid/Dream-RSI)
- [Authors' project website](https://www.dream-rsi.com)

The research authors did not author this implementation. This project is not
endorsed by or affiliated with Google, Google DeepMind, or the authors' institutions.
The upstream paper, institutional logos, branded figures, README, and original
citation file are deliberately excluded from this distribution. Its MIT license
applies to the independent implementation distributed here, not to upstream works.

Ollama, Docker/Colima, Python container images, and model weights are external
requirements, installed separately under their respective licenses. They are not
bundled or relicensed by this repository. Optional plotting uses matplotlib.
